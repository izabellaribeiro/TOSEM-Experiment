# Study inputs

- `raw/`: original uploaded story datasets, unchanged.
- `processed/`: canonical story texts and line-to-story ID maps used by all current execution scripts; these are distinct from the uploaded originals.
- `ground_truth/`: final human reference labels and their provenance note.
- `qualitative_sample/`: RQ4 coder and adjudication workbooks, not Gold Standard construction workbooks.

See [traceability](../docs/TRACEABILITY.md) for the stage/RQ mapping and [the inventory](../docs/provenance/artifact_inventory.csv) for original paths and hashes. No annotation or dataset content was edited during organization.
